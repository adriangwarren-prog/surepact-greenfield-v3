## 2. Project Proposal

### 2.1 Project Title
**Urapuntja Elder Care Navigation & Trusted Facilitator Program**

### 2.2 Project Summary
The Urapuntja Elder Care program aims to address the significant under-representation of Utopia homelands residents in aged care support. Due to language barriers, distrust of institutional frameworks, and lack of local support, remote Elders face extreme difficulties navigating My Aged Care. UHSAC will employ 1.5 FTE Trusted Indigenous Facilitators (Care Navigators) from the local community to identify vulnerable Elders, conduct home visits, complete assessments, and coordinate Home Care Packages (HCP) so they can remain healthy on Country.

### 2.3 Objectives
1. Identify and register 50 remote Utopia Elders on the My Aged Care portal.
2. Conduct culturally safe, bilingual home assessments at 16 outstations.
3. Coordinate delivery of domestic help, clinical nursing, and nutrition support to approved Elders.
4. Deliver training on aged care systems to local community-controlled workers.

### 2.4 Project Deliverables
* 1.5 FTE trained local Elder Care Facilitators.
* Local register of Utopia Elders, their current My Aged Care status, and care needs.
* Outreach support guidelines for remote aged care assessors visiting the region.
