## 2. Project Proposal

### 2.1 Project Title
**Soapy Bore Primary Health Clinic Water Quality and Filtration Upgrade**

### 2.2 Project Summary
The primary health clinic at Amengernterne (Soapy Bore) relies on local underground bore water. High mineral content (calcium carbonate, silica) and intermittent microbiological contamination pose a severe health risk to staff, vulnerable renal patients, and expectant mothers. This project requests $250,000 to purchase and install a commercial-grade, solar-powered reverse osmosis (RO) water filtration system, replace the aging 50,000-litre storage tanks, and implement a monthly chemical/microbiological water testing regime.

### 2.3 Objectives
1. Provide constant access to safe, WHO-compliant drinking water at the central clinic.
2. Reduce scale buildup in clinic medical equipment, extending operational life.
3. Install an off-grid solar-battery unit to run the RO system during mains power outages.
4. Train local maintenance staff in basic filter cleaning and routine testing.

### 2.4 Project Deliverables
* Solar-powered commercial Reverse Osmosis system (capacity 5,000 L/day).
* 2x 25,000L heavy-duty polyethylene water storage tanks.
* 10kW ground-mounted solar array + 15kWh lithium battery storage.
* Certified water quality test reports confirming removal of impurities.
