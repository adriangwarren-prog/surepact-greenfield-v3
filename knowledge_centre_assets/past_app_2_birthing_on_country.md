## 2. Project Proposal

### 2.1 Project Title
**Building capacity of regional and remote ACCHOs for Birthing on Country Initiatives and augmented smoking cessation care through University-ACCHO partnerships**

### 2.2 Project Summary
UHSAC in partnership with Southern Cross University proposes a collaborative action research model to design and implement a culturally integrated "Birthing on Country" model in the Utopia Homelands. The program aims to establish maternal health services that respect traditional lore and practice, while integrating clinical midwifery care. Additionally, the project will embed augmented smoking cessation programs tailored specifically for expectant First Nations mothers in remote regions, aiming to reduce low birthweight and premature births.

### 2.3 Objectives
1. Recruit and train 2.0 FTE local Aboriginal Health Practitioners (AHPs) in maternal health support.
2. Establish a co-designed, culturally appropriate antenatal clinic facility at Soapy Bore.
3. Reduce smoking rates among pregnant Alyawarr and Anmatyerr women by 30% over three years.
4. Establish formal clinical escalation pathways with Alice Springs Hospital for high-risk pregnancies.

### 2.4 Project Deliverables
* Co-designed antenatal care guidelines combining clinical midwifery and traditional doula/grandmother support.
* Local training program for maternal health workers.
* Culturally safe education brochures and carbon monoxide monitoring units.
