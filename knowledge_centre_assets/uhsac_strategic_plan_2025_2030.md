# UHSAC Strategic Plan 2025-2030

## 1. Mission and Values
* **Mission**: To deliver high-quality, culturally safe, self-determined primary healthcare to the Urapuntja homelands community, empowering our people to live healthy and strong on Country.
* **Vision**: A healthy, thriving, and self-reliant Utopia homelands where clinical excellence and traditional culture walk hand-in-hand.
* **Core Values**: Cultural Integrity, Respect, Community Control, Accountability, and Equity.

## 2. Strategic Pillars and Objectives

### Pillar 1: Clinical Excellence & Homelands Outreach
* **Objective**: Ensure that all residents of the 16 outstations have regular, equitable access to primary healthcare.
* **Action**: Expand mobile outreach frequency, upgrade clinical telemetry for remote testing, and maintain a robust GP and nursing roster.

### Pillar 2: First Nations Workforce Development
* **Objective**: Build a sustainable, local Aboriginal health workforce.
* **Action**: Create clear training pathways for Aboriginal Health Practitioners (AHPs), Health Promotion Officers, and Care Navigators within the Utopia community.

### Pillar 3: Cultural Integration & Birthing on Country
* **Objective**: Integrate cultural protocols and traditional knowledge into clinical programs.
* **Action**: Deliver the Southern Cross University Birthing on Country partnership, train midwives in cultural safety, and establish a dedicated cultural birthing suite at Soapy Bore.

### Pillar 4: Infrastructure & Digital Health Resilience
* **Objective**: Upgrade clinics, housing, and IT networks to support modern medical services.
* **Action**: Invest in stable satellite broadband, off-grid solar energy systems, and clinic structural expansions.

### Pillar 5: Governance & Financial Sustainability
* **Objective**: Maintain the highest standards of corporate governance and financial transparency.
* **Action**: Implement independent internal audits, strengthen risk assessment procedures, and diversify funding streams.
