## Part B: Standard Grant Conditions

### 1. Purpose of the Grant
The Grantee must use the Grant Funding solely to perform the Activity described in this Agreement. Any unauthorized diversion of funds will constitute a material breach and trigger immediate recovery procedures.

### 2. Assets and Equipment
Any equipment or assets purchased with the Grant Funding (including Starlink hardware, water tanks, solar panels, and switchboards) must be:
- Registered on the Grantee's asset register.
- Properly maintained, insured, and used exclusively for the emergency relief and clinic operations of the Utopia Homelands.
- Retained under the ownership of the community-controlled organization for a minimum of five (5) years post-project completion.

### 3. Record Keeping and Audit
The Grantee must maintain complete and accurate financial records relating to the receipt and expenditure of the Grant Funding. NEMA or its authorized representatives reserve the right to audit the Grantee's records and inspect the project sites at Soapy Bore and outer outstations upon seven (7) days' notice.
