---

## 2. Program Goals and Scope
Utopia homelands consist of 16 dispersed outstations. Many residents lack transport to travel to the central clinic at Soapy Bore, leading to untreated chronic conditions and low primary care access. The Mobile Health Outreach Program delivers a fully equipped "clinic on wheels" directly to outstations.

### Objectives:
1. Conduct scheduled weekly clinical outreach visits to all 16 outstations.
2. Complete 2,500 clinical presentations annually in outstation communities.
3. Deliver primary medication refills, childhood immunisations, and chronic disease testing.
4. Establish satellite-linked point-of-care diagnostics for immediate testing.

## 3. Weekly Mobile Clinic Rotation Schedule

| Day | Homelands Visited | Focus Services |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Arlparra, Camel Camp | Child health checks, school immunisations |
| **Tuesday** | Apungalindum (Crows Nest), Mulberry Bore | Aged care home checks, diabetes testing |
| **Wednesday** | Irrultja, Mosquito Bore | Antenatal checks, chronic disease reviews |
| **Thursday** | Soakage Bore, Kurrajong | GP clinics, pharmaceutical dispensing |
| **Friday** | Three Bores, Outer Outstations | Emergency follow-ups, equipment maintenance |

*Note: Schedule varies during November-March (wet season) based on Sandover Highway flooding conditions.*

---

## 4. Vehicle Specifications & Clinical Capabilities
The outreach vehicle is custom-designed for extreme remote operations:
* **Chassis**: Heavy-duty off-road 4WD chassis with high ground clearance, bullbar, and winch.
* **Clinical Bay**: Air-conditioned, dust-proof consulting room with medical examination couch, refrigerator for vaccine storage, and running water.
* **Point-of-Care Lab**: In-vehicle diagnostics for blood glucose, HbA1c, lipids, and urinalysis.
* **Communications**: Starlink Flat High Performance satellite terminal for real-time electronic health records (Communicare) access and telehealth video links.
* **Power Supply**: 2.4kW solar array + 10kWh lithium battery bank with auto-starting backup generator to run HVAC and vaccine fridge continuously.
