# UHSAC Corporate Capability Statement

## 1. Executive Summary
Urapuntja Health Service Aboriginal Corporation (UHSAC) is a mature, fully accredited Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisation (ACCHO) based in the Utopia Homelands of the Northern Territory. With a 40-year track record of delivering primary clinical care and infrastructure projects in extreme remote conditions, UHSAC possesses the clinical expertise, operational logistics, and governance frameworks required to successfully execute state and federal programs.

## 2. Core Capabilities

### 2.1 Remote Clinical Delivery
* **Central Facility**: Fully operational primary care clinic at Soapy Bore, containing three consulting bays, pharmacy, emergency resuscitation room, and dental suite.
* **Outreach Operations**: Purpose-built 4WD mobile medical van delivering weekly clinical care to 16 remote outstations.
* **Accreditation**: Fully accredited under AGPAL standards and ISO 9001 quality management systems.
* **Electronic Health Records**: Utilises Communicare for secure, real-time patient clinical records, with remote synchronization via Starlink.

### 2.2 Project Management & Infrastructure
* **Capital Works Track Record**: Successfully managed projects including clinic refits, water filtration upgrades ($250k), and staff housing builds.
* **Subcontractor Management**: Strong relationships with regional civil works companies and remote trades.
* **Compliance**: Strict adherence to NT building codes, environmental EPA standards, and occupational health regulations.

### 2.3 Cultural Governance & Engagement
* **Indigenous Governance**: Community-elected Board of Directors ensuring self-determination and alignment with local lore.
* **Bilingual Workforce**: Over 40% of staff are local Alyawarr and Anmatyerr speakers, providing translation and cultural support.
* **Community Trust**: Long-term presence in the Utopia homelands has fostered high trust, ensuring high clinical engagement rates.

## 3. Past Performance Proof-Points

* **Birthing on Country Partnership (with Southern Cross University & DoHAC)**: Co-designed remote antenatal care and smoking cessation guidelines, improving child health checks to 88%.
* **Cancer Workforce and Screening Initiative (with NACCHO)**: Recruited and trained 1.0 FTE Health Promotion Officer and 2.0 FTE Cancer Support Officers to coordinate remote oncology care.
* **Trusted Indigenous Facilitators Program (with NACCHO)**: Deployed Care Navigators across Utopia, assisting 45+ Elders in accessing My Aged Care services.
* **Soapy Bore Clinic Water Upgrade**: Managed the installation of a solar-powered reverse osmosis water filtration system, securing safe clinical water supply.
