## 2. Project Proposal

### 2.1 Project Title
**Soapy Bore Crows - Uniform and Health Promotion Initiative**

### 2.2 Project Summary
The Soapy Bore Crows Football Club, representing the Utopia Homelands, requests $1,000.00 to purchase new official club uniforms, equipment, and to run community health promotion sessions alongside home match fixtures. As an remote football club, the Crows serve as a key social hub for the Alyawarr and Anmatyerr youth. By partnering with UHSAC, we will leverage local footy games to conduct child health checks, distribute health literature, and promote healthy lifestyles to combat remote health challenges.

### 2.3 Objectives
1. Provide safe, high-quality uniforms and training footballs for 25 local First Nations players.
2. Conduct at least 3 clinical outreach health desks at Utopia football match days.
3. Promote physical activity, teamwork, and community engagement in remote outstations.

### 2.4 Project Deliverables
* 25x Sublimated playing jerseys with Soapy Bore Crows logo.
* 10x AFL leather training footballs.
* 3x Health promotion banners for clinic and ground display.
