## 2. Project Proposal

### 2.1 Project Title
**Utopia Cancer Care Coordination and Screening Mobilisation Project**

### 2.2 Project Summary
UHSAC proposes to establish a dedicated Cancer Outreach and Care Unit in the Utopia homelands. Remote First Nations communities experience low cancer screening rates and delayed diagnosis, leading to poor survival outcomes. By deploying 1.0 FTE Health Promotion Officer to conduct local education and 2.0 FTE Cancer Support Officers to manage treatment coordination and family support, we will bridge the gap between tertiary oncology centers in Alice Springs/Adelaide and remote homelands. The requested $320,000 will fund targeted communication campaigns and travel support.

### 2.3 Objectives
1. Increase participation in breast, bowel, and cervical cancer screenings by 40% across Utopia.
2. Provide local, culturally safe care coordination for patients undergoing active oncology treatment.
3. Conduct 12 monthly community education workshops in local outstations.
4. Supply transport assistance for patients traveling to Alice Springs Hospital for oncology.

### 2.4 Project Deliverables
* Regional cancer screening communication materials translated into Alyawarr and Anmatyerr languages.
* Outreach screening clinic schedules (in partnership with BreastScreen NT).
* Individual care plan template for remote cancer patients.
