# UHSAC Healthy Communities 10-Year Masterplan (2026-2036)

## 1. Vision for 2036
By 2036, the Utopia homelands will possess a modern, highly resilient, and fully integrated healthcare network. Local residents will have access to clinical services, clean water, reliable clean power, and culturally appropriate aged care without needing to leave their traditional lands.

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Development Map

### Phase I: Foundation & Essential Infrastructure (Years 1-3: 2026-2029)
* **Clinic Expansion**: Expand the Soapy Bore Clinic to include a dedicated pediatric wing, a birthing suite, and private consulting rooms.
* **Clean Water Security**: Install solar-powered reverse osmosis water filtration units at Soapy Bore and the four largest outstations.
* **Emergency Power**: Install solar microgrids with lithium battery storage at critical outstations to prevent vaccine spoilage and power clinic electronics during grid outages.

### Phase II: Workforce & Outreach Integration (Years 4-6: 2029-2032)
* **Aboriginal Health Practitioner (AHP) Hub**: Transition outstation clinics to AHP-led models, backed by telehealth links to Soapy Bore.
* **Mobile Outreach Fleet**: Upgrade the outreach fleet to include two 4WD clinical trucks equipped with sat-comms and basic emergency treatment bays.
* **Staff Housing**: Construct four modern, energy-efficient staff houses at Soapy Bore to attract and retain senior medical staff.

### Phase III: Comprehensive Care & Autonomy (Years 7-10: 2032-2036)
* **Utopia Aged Care Facility**: Construct a 6-bed culturally safe respite and low-care residential facility for Elders.
* **Self-Determination Hub**: Complete transition of all administrative, finance, and program management roles to local community members.

## 3. Financial Forecast and Funding Needs
Total estimated capital works and program costs over 10 years: **$18,500,000**.
Targeted funding mix:
* 60% Commonwealth Capital Works Grants
* 25% Northern Territory Government Infrastructure Fund
* 15% Philanthropic and Corporate Sponsorship (e.g. ARENA, Telstra, Minderoo)
