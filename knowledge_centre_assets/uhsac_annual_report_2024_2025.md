# UHSAC Annual Report 2024-2025

## 1. CEO Overview
The 2024-2025 financial year marked a significant expansion in our staffing capacity and preventative health programs. A major highlight was our successful funding offer from NACCHO for the Cancer Workforce and Screening program, which enabled the recruitment of local Health Promotion and Cancer Support officers. We also established a dedicated Elder Care navigation unit.

## 2. Clinical and Health Performance
During the 2024-2025 reporting period:
* **Total Clinical Encounters**: 12,850 presentations (12.5% increase from previous year).
* **Active Patient Count**: 1,410 unique patients serviced.
* **Immunisation Rate (Aged 0-5)**: 94% fully vaccinated.
* **Aged Care Navigation Clients**: 45 Elders supported in accessing My Aged Care.
* **Cancer Screenings Facilitated**: 68 breast, bowel, and cervical screenings coordinated.

## 3. Key Achievements
* **Cancer Program Mobilisation**: Recruited 1.0 FTE Health Promotion Officer and 2.0 FTE Cancer Support Officers under the NACCHO Cancer Program.
* **Aged Care Expansion**: Commenced delivery of the Trusted Indigenous Facilitators Program, employing local staff to support Utopia Elders.
* **Outreach Caravan Purchase**: Commissioned a newly retrofitted off-road Mobile Health Van to replace the aging Toyota Troopcarrier.
