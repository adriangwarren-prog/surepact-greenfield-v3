# UHSAC Annual Report 2023-2024

## 1. President & CEO Address
The 2023-24 financial year has been one of consolidation and strategic alignment for UHSAC. Under the guidance of our Board, we have focused on expanding primary health services to the outer homelands, addressing high chronic disease burden, and securing critical partnerships. We are proud of our ongoing collaboration with Southern Cross University to implement the "Birthing on Country" model.

## 2. Clinical and Health Performance
During the 2023-2024 reporting period, UHSAC achieved:
* **Total Clinical Encounters**: 11,420 presentations across all clinics and outreach.
* **Active Patient Count**: 1,320 unique patients serviced.
* **Chronic Disease Management Plans**: 450 active care plans.
* **Child Health Checks (715)**: 88% coverage of children aged 0-5 years.
* **Mobile Outreach Visits**: 142 scheduled outstation clinics conducted.

## 3. Key Achievements
* **Birthing on Country Partnership**: Executed the Collaborative Research Agreement with Southern Cross University in September 2023, initiating local maternal health training.
* **Emergency Water System**: Secured a minor grant to restore the reverse osmosis filtration plant at Soapy Bore clinic, ensuring clean drinking water for staff and patients.
* **Clinical Equipment Upgrades**: Acquired three new digital ECG units and portable ultrasound machines.
