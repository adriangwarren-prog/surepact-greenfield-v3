# Urapuntja Health Service Aboriginal Corporation (UHSAC) - Corporate Profile

## 1. Executive Summary
Urapuntja Health Service Aboriginal Corporation (UHSAC) is a First Nations community-controlled health organisation (ACCHO) established in 1986. UHSAC delivers comprehensive primary health care services to the residents of the Utopia Homelands (Urapuntja) in the Barkly Region of the Northern Territory. 

UHSAC operates under a community-elected Board of Directors representing the various homelands, ensuring that healthcare delivery is culturally safe, self-determined, and aligned with local needs.

## 3. Operational Geography
UHSAC serves a highly dispersed population of approximately 1,200 to 1,500 Aboriginal people (primarily Anmatyerr and Alyawarr speakers) living across 16 outstations (homelands) in the Utopia region. This covers an area of roughly 10,000 square kilometres.
Key outstations served:
1. **Amengernterne (Soapy Bore)** - Central clinic location
2. **Apungalindum (Crows Nest)** - Outstation clinic and local football ground
3. **Arlparra** - Main regional hub with store and school
4. **Camel Camp** - Homeland settlement
5. **Irrultja** - Easternmost outstation clinic
6. **Mosquito Bore** - Homeland settlement
7. **Mulberry Bore** - Homeland settlement
8. **Soakage Bore** - Homeland settlement
9. **Kurrajong** - Homeland settlement
10. **Three Bores** - Homeland settlement

## 4. Services Offered
* **Primary Health Care Clinic**: Located at Soapy Bore, offering GP services, nursing care, emergency response, and pharmacy dispensing.
* **Mobile Outreach Clinic**: Dedicated health van visiting 16 outstations on a scheduled weekly rotation.
* **Maternal and Child Health**: Birthing on Country initiatives, antenatal/postnatal care, and immunisation.
* **Chronic Disease Management**: Diabetes, cardiovascular health, and renal support.
* **Aged Care & Disability Support**: Home Care Packages, navigation support, and Elder care.
* **Mental Health & Social Wellbeing**: Culturally safe counselling and alcohol/drug support.
