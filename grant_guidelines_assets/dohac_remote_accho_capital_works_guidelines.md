# DoHAC Remote ACCHO Clinic Capital Works Program

## 1. Program Overview
* **Funding Agency**: Commonwealth Department of Health and Aged Care (DoHAC)
* **Program Name**: Remote Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisation Capital Works Fund
* **Goal**: To modernise and expand primary clinical infrastructure in remote areas, enabling remote ACCHOs to deliver comprehensive, culturally safe healthcare and integrate specialized medical services.
* **Funding Value**: Grants between $250,000 and $1,500,000.
* **Funding Term**: Up to 2 years from agreement execution.

---

## 2. Program Priorities
Priority is given to projects that:
1. **Expand Clinical Space**: Construct additional consultation rooms to support visiting allied health, GPs, and specialized programs (e.g. maternal health, dental, and mental health).
2. **Improve Infection Control**: Upgrade ventilation, waiting areas, and isolation bays to meet modern hygiene and infection control standards.
3. **Enhance Digital Telehealth**: Install digital infrastructure, telecommunication links, and point-of-care lab stations in remote hubs.

---

## 3. Required Application Sections

Applicants must provide detailed written responses to the following components:

### Section A: Project Description & Clinical Objectives
* *Questions*:
  1. Describe the proposed clinical expansion or refurbishment. What additional rooms or systems will be constructed?
  2. How will this expansion improve patient throughput, clinical safety, or access to specialists in your remote region?
  3. Detail how the design respects local cultural protocols (e.g. gender-separated waiting areas, cultural birth options, private consultation layout).

### Section B: Technical Specifications & Risk Management
* *Questions*:
  1. Provide the structural engineering and architectural approach (e.g., prefab modular units, on-site traditional construction).
  2. Outline your risk mitigation plan for remote building construction, specifically focusing on extreme weather (monsoonal wet season), logistics delays, and equipment commissioning.
  3. Detail how medical waste, water supply, and power reliability will be managed in the expanded clinic.

### Section C: Project Budget & Implementation Timeline
* *Questions*:
  1. Provide a detailed cost breakdown grouped by key construction phases (Consulting, Ground Works, Structure, Electrical/HVAC, Fit-out).
  2. Present a 12-to-24 month project schedule with clear milestone targets.
  3. Detail the project manager's qualification and the organisation's financial oversight capability.
